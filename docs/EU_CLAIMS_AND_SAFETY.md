# EU claims and safety: use with caution

The linked [EU communications table](../data/assessments/eu_claims_audit.csv) and [safety table](../data/assessments/safety_limits.csv) preserve the source audit's preliminary research notes. **Neither is a verified legal register, formulation clearance, label sign-off or medical recommendation.**

Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 governs nutrition and health claims for foods in the EU. A peer-reviewed paper does **not** authorize a claim. Specific nutrient, source, wording, daily intake and warning requirements must be checked against the current official EU register and relevant national requirements.

Important distinctions in this research index:
- Authorized claims relating to nutrients do not automatically apply to a specific brand, salt, dose or finished formulation.
- Disease treatment/prevention, dementia prevention, reversal of biological age and lifespan extension must not be inferred from surrogate biomarkers or preliminary studies.
- The `eu_claims_audit.csv` column titled "Lower-risk authorized territory" summarizes potential claim categories from the research source and **does not confirm eligibility for any actual product or dosage**.
- Clinically meaningful safety and interaction information must be verified from full sources and by a qualified practitioner.

Official reference: https://ec.europa.eu/food/food-feed-portal/screen/health-claims/eu-register

No interpretation in this repository constitutes compliance approval.
